How it works

  1. Set frequenciesHow often should each piece come up? 100% means every day, 50% every other day. Fix some to specific days if they must fall there.
  2. GeneratePractiae spreads the pieces evenly across your cycle, keeps fixed days fixed, and respects your exclusion and inclusion groups.
  3. PractiseFollow the day's list. The tag chart shows the mix at a glance, so no day is all scales and no day is all repertoire.
